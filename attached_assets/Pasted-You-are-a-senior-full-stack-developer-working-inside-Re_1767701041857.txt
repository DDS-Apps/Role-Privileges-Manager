NEW BUSINESS CONCEPT
1) Legal Ownership
- Every manager has ONE legal company.
- Every employee is legally employed by ONE legal company.
- A manager can ONLY modify privileges for employees who belong to the manager’s legal company (i.e., “his legal employees”).

2) Cross-company privileges
- Even though a manager can only manage his legal employees, he can grant them privileges in ANY company by selecting a “Company Context” dropdown.
- This means the employee remains legally under the manager’s company, but can be assigned privileges for other companies.
